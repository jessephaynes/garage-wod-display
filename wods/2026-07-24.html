Part A — Warm-Up

  • 2 rounds
  • 300 m Row
  • 10 PVC/banded pass-throughs
  • 8 Empty bar muscle cleans
  • 8 Front squats (empty bar)
  • 6 Push presses (empty bar)
  • 5 Strict pull-ups or 8 banded pull-downs
  • 3 progressive clean & jerk sets of 3 reps

Part B — Strength

  • Clean & Jerk
  • 5 sets of 2 reps @ 205 lb
  • Rest 2–3 minutes between sets

Focus: Fast elbows, vertical dip, aggressive drive, quick footwork.

Intent: Accumulate crisp heavy doubles without approaching a max.

Part C — MetCon

  • 18-minute AMRAP
  • 12/10 Cal Echo Bike
  • 9 Power Cleans 135 lb
  • 12 Box Jump Overs 24 in
  • 9 Chest-to-Bar Pull-Ups
  • Scale cleans to 95–115 lb as needed
  • Scale pull-ups to ring rows as needed

Stimulus: Steady aerobic effort with repeatable barbell cycling.

Goal: Complete 5–7 rounds while maintaining consistent pacing.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 15 GHD Hip Extensions
  • 12 Single-arm DB Bench Press per side
  • 20 Banded Face Pulls
  • 1:00 Front Rack Stretch

Accessory Intent: Improve upper-back endurance, trunk stability, and front rack mobility.
