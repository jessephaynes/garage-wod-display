Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m Echo Row or Ski
  • 10 PVC pass-throughs
  • 10 empty bar strict press
  • 5 push press
  • 5 push jerk
  • 5 split jerk alternating lead leg
  • 30 single-unders
  • 20 double-under practice attempts
  • 2–3 barbell warm-up sets

Part B — Strength

  • Every 2:30 x 7 sets
  • 2 Push Press
  • 2 Push Jerk
  • 2 Split Jerk
  • Build across sets with perfect technique

Focus: Vertical dip, aggressive drive, stable lockout, and consistent footwork.

Intent: Develop confidence and efficiency through the shoulder-to-overhead progression.

Part C — MetCon

  • 15-Minute AMRAP
  • 40 Double-Unders
  • 10 Shoulder-to-Overhead 115 lb
  • 12 Box Jump Overs 24 in
  • 250m Row

Stimulus: Moderate barbell cycling with elevated heart rate and jump rope skill.

Goal: Complete 5–6 rounds while keeping the barbell mostly unbroken.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 15 GHD hip extensions
  • 15 banded face pulls
  • 45-second front rack stretch
  • 45-second calf stretch

Accessory Intent: Reinforce posterior chain, shoulder health, and mobility after overhead work.
