Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 1:00 easy Echo Bike
  • 10 air squats
  • 8 alternating reverse lunges
  • 8 banded good mornings
  • 5 inchworms
  • 5 empty bar muscle cleans
  • 5 empty bar front squats
  • 3 x 10-second Echo Bike builds (70% → 80% → 90%)
  • 50 seconds rest between efforts

Part B — Strength

  • Power Clean
  • 2 reps @ 185 lb
  • 2 reps @ 205 lb
  • 2 reps @ 205 lb
  • 2 reps @ 215 lb
  • 2 reps @ 215 lb
  • Optional: 2 reps @ 225 lb if bar speed is excellent

Focus: Fast turnover and aggressive extension.

Intent: Prime the nervous system without accumulating fatigue.

Part C — MetCon

  • 10 rounds
  • 15-second all-out Echo Bike sprint
  • 6 sandbag over shoulder (150 lb)
  • Use 8 reps if using the 100 lb sandbag
  • Rest until 1:30 has elapsed before the next round

Stimulus: Repeat sprint power with full commitment every round.

Goal: Keep bike output consistent and every sandbag rep explosive.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 12 GHD hip extensions
  • 15 ring rows
  • 20 banded face pulls
  • 1:00 front rack stretch

Accessory Intent: Build upper back endurance, posterior chain strength, and restore front rack mobility.
