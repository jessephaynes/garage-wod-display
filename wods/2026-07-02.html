Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m row or SkiErg
  • 8 air squats
  • 6 inchworms with push-up
  • 10 banded pass-throughs
  • 10 empty-bar front rack good mornings
  • 5 strict hanging knee raises
  • 2 rounds movement prep
  • 5 empty-bar front squats
  • 5 push press
  • 5 push jerks
  • 3 box jump step-downs
  • 3 toes-to-bar

Part B — Strength

  • Front Squat
  • 5 sets of 3 reps
  • 195 lb
  • 225 lb
  • 245 lb
  • 260 lb
  • 275 lb
  • Rest 2–3 minutes between sets

Focus: Stay tall through the torso, keep elbows high, and drive aggressively from the bottom.

Intent: Heavy but technically sound triples with no grinders.

Part C — MetCon

  • 18-minute AMRAP
  • 15 box jumps (24/20 in)
  • 12 shoulder-to-overhead (115/95 lb)
  • 9 toes-to-bar

Stimulus: Moderate-to-high intensity with steady pacing and sustainable barbell cycling.

Goal: Maintain consistent rounds by managing grip and shoulder fatigue.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 12 GHD hip extensions
  • 15 dumbbell hammer curls
  • 20 banded face pulls
  • 1:00 front rack stretch
  • 1:00 lat stretch each side

Accessory Intent: Build posterior chain endurance while restoring shoulder and front rack mobility.
