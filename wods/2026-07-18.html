Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 8 empty-bar front squats
  • 8 empty-bar push presses
  • 8 empty-bar thrusters
  • 8 lateral bar hops
  • 8 GHD hip extensions
  • 5 GHD sit-ups
  • 10 Echo Bike calories at an easy pace
  • Build to a 135 lb thruster in 3–4 quick sets
  • Practice one smooth barbell weight change

Part B — Strength

  • Thruster primer
  • 5 reps at 75 lb
  • 5 reps at 95 lb
  • 3 reps at 115 lb
  • 2 reps at 135 lb
  • Rest 60–90 seconds between sets

Focus: Keep the bar stacked over the midfoot and finish each rep with an aggressive leg drive.

Intent: Prepare the exact loading progression without creating fatigue before the MetCon.

Part C — MetCon

  • 18-minute total clock
  • First 6-minute window
  • 30 thrusters at 95 lb
  • 30 lateral bar burpees
  • 30 GHD sit-ups
  • Change the bar to 115 lb before biking
  • Max Echo Bike calories in the remaining time
  • Immediately begin the second 6-minute window
  • 20 thrusters at 115 lb
  • 30 lateral bar burpees
  • 30 GHD sit-ups
  • Change the bar to 135 lb before biking
  • Max Echo Bike calories in the remaining time
  • Immediately begin the third 6-minute window
  • 10 thrusters at 135 lb
  • 30 lateral bar burpees
  • 30 GHD sit-ups
  • Max Echo Bike calories in the remaining time
  • Score is total Echo Bike calories

Stimulus: A high-output conditioning test that rewards controlled barbell cycling, uninterrupted movement, and aggressive bike finishes.

Goal: Reach the bike in every window. Stay conservative in the first six minutes, protect the middle window, and empty the tank in the final bike effort.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 5 minutes easy Echo Bike or walking
  • 2 rounds
  • 60-second couch stretch per side
  • 60-second banded lat stretch per side
  • 30-second dead hang

Accessory Intent: Downshift the heart rate and restore the quads, hip flexors, shoulders, and trunk after the high-volume work.
