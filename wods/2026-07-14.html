Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 8/6 cal Echo Bike
  • 8 alternating Samson lunges
  • 8 scap pull-ups
  • 6 kip swings
  • 5 burpees over an empty bar
  • Then 2 rounds with an empty bar
  • 5 clean deadlifts
  • 5 clean high pulls
  • 5 hang muscle cleans
  • 5 front squats
  • 3 tall squat cleans

Part B — Strength

  • Every 2:00 x 9 sets
  • 1 squat clean
  • Set 1 — 160 lb
  • Set 2 — 170 lb
  • Set 3 — 180 lb
  • Set 4 — 190 lb
  • Set 5 — 200 lb
  • Set 6 — 210 lb
  • Set 7 — 220 lb
  • Set 8 — 225–235 lb
  • Set 9 — 225–245 lb if technically sharp

Focus: Finish the leg drive, keep the bar close, and meet the bar aggressively in the bottom.

Intent: Build from roughly 60% toward 92% of a 265 lb clean and jerk 1RM while preserving sharp squat clean mechanics.

Part C — MetCon

  • 18-minute AMRAP
  • 15 cal Echo Bike
  • 12 toes-to-bar
  • 9 bar-facing burpees over the bar
  • 6 squat cleans
  • 155 lb

Stimulus: A repeatable conditioning loop with increasing movement interference. The bike elevates the heart rate before trunk, grip, and breathing fatigue collide with moderately loaded squat cleans.

Goal: Complete 5–6 rounds with consistent splits. Bike at a firm 80–85%. Keep toes-to-bar unbroken or use 7+5. Move steadily through burpees. Perform the squat cleans as quick, disciplined singles.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3–4 rounds
  • 10 GHD hip extensions
  • 12 DB bench press
  • 50 lb dumbbells
  • 15 banded face pulls
  • 40–60 sec easy SkiErg
  • Rest 60 sec between rounds

Accessory Intent: Build posterior-chain durability and upper-body structural strength without adding meaningful quad or grip fatigue.
