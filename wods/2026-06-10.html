Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m Row
  • 10 pass-throughs or band shoulder openers
  • 8 empty-bar front squats
  • 8 box step-ups (24")
  • 5 strict pull-ups or ring rows
  • Then 2 rounds
  • 5 front squats @ 95 lb
  • 3 push jerks @ 95 lb
  • 2 hang power cleans @ 95 lb

Part B — Strength

  • Front Squat
  • 5 reps @ 185 lb
  • 5 reps @ 205 lb
  • 4 reps @ 225 lb
  • 3 reps @ 255 lb
  • 3 reps @ 275 lb
  • If 275 moves exceptionally well, perform a second set of 3 at 275 lb.
  • Optional: Front Rack Hold @ 275 lb for 20–30 seconds.

Focus: Aggressive elbows, strong brace, and a vertical torso through the sticking point.

Intent: Accumulate quality strength work without excessive fatigue and finish feeling powerful rather than depleted.

Part C — MetCon

  • 5 rounds for time
  • 12 Cal Echo Bike
  • 9 Front Squats (135 lb)
  • 6 Bar-Facing Burpees
  • 3 Muscle-Ups
  • Substitution: 6 Chest-to-Bar Pull-Ups or 9 Pull-Ups

Stimulus: Fast-paced conditioning where the bike elevates heart rate, front squats challenge recovery, and gymnastics demand composure under fatigue.

Goal: Advanced 12–15 minutes, Intermediate 15–18 minutes. Keep front squats unbroken and maintain consistent round times.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 4 sets
  • 12 GHD Hip Extensions
  • 15 GHD Sit-Ups
  • 30-second Sandbag Bear Hug Hold (150/100 lb)
  • Rest 90 seconds
  • Accumulate 3:00 Hollow Hold
  • Accumulate 3:00 Side Plank total

Accessory Intent: Develop trunk stiffness, front-rack support, and posterior-chain endurance to support future squatting and Olympic lifting.
