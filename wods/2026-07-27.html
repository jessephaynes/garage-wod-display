Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m Row
  • 8 Empty Bar Thrusters
  • 5 Burpees
  • 5 Front Squats
  • 5 Strict Pull-Ups or Ring Rows
  • 2 rounds
  • 5 Thrusters @ 95 lb
  • 5 Bar-Facing Burpees
  • 6 Echo Bike Calories

Part B — Strength

  • Every 90 seconds x 8 sets
  • 1 Power Clean
  • 1 Front Squat
  • Build to 235–255 lb
  • Optional final attempt 260–265 lb

Focus: Fast elbows, aggressive pull under, smooth transition into the squat.

Intent: Prime the nervous system without accumulating fatigue.

Part C — MetCon

  • 5 rounds for time
  • 12 Echo Bike Calories
  • 8 Thrusters @ 135 lb
  • 6 Bar-Facing Burpees
  • Time cap: 8:00

Stimulus: True sprint with controlled aggression.

Goal: Finish in 5:30–7:00 with unbroken thrusters and quick transitions.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 12 GHD Hip Extensions
  • 15 Banded Face Pulls
  • 45-second Front Rack Stretch
  • 1-minute Easy Bike

Accessory Intent: Restore the upper back and posterior chain while flushing the legs.
