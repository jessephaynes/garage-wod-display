Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m Row
  • 8 Empty-bar muscle cleans
  • 8 Empty-bar front squats
  • 6 Empty-bar push presses
  • 5 Strict pull-ups or 8 banded pull-downs
  • 30-second ankle and front rack stretch

Part B — Strength

  • Front Squat
  • 5 reps @ 205 lb
  • 5 reps @ 225 lb
  • 3 reps @ 245 lb
  • 3 reps @ 260 lb
  • 3+ reps @ 275 lb

Focus: Stay upright, keep elbows high, and drive through the floor.

Intent: Build leg strength without grinding to failure.

Part C — MetCon

  • For time
  • 18-minute cap
  • 4 rounds
  • 500m Row
  • 12 Bar-facing burpees
  • 9 Front squats @ 135 lb
  • 6 Chest-to-bar pull-ups

Stimulus: Sustained engine with consistent leg output and manageable gymnastics.

Goal: Finish in 13–18 minutes with even pacing.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 12 GHD hip extensions
  • 12 Single-arm dumbbell bench press each arm
  • 50/35 lb dumbbell
  • 15 Banded face pulls
  • 1-minute front rack stretch

Accessory Intent: Improve upper-back endurance, pressing stability, posterior-chain strength, and front rack mobility.
