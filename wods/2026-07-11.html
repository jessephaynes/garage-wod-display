Part A — Warm-Up

  • 2 rounds
  • 300m Easy Row
  • 10 Walking Lunges
  • 10 Scap Pull-Ups
  • 8 Empty-Bar Hang Power Cleans
  • 6 Empty-Bar Front Squats
  • 4 Empty-Bar Push Jerks
  • 3 progressive sets of 3 Power Cleans

Part B — Strength

  • Every 2:00 x 9 sets
  • 1 Power Clean
  • 1 Hang Squat Clean
  • 1 Front Squat
  • 1 Split Jerk
  • Set 1: 155 lb
  • Set 2: 165 lb
  • Set 3: 175 lb
  • Set 4: 185 lb
  • Set 5: 195 lb
  • Set 6: 205 lb
  • Final 3 sets: build by feel

Focus: Smooth positions and quality footwork.

Intent: Finish heavy while keeping every complex crisp and avoiding a max-out effort.

Part C — MetCon

  • For time
  • 1,000m Row
  • 30 Toes-to-Bar
  • 20 Barbell Front-Rack Walking Lunges @ 95 lb
  • 15 Bar-Facing Burpees
  • 10 C2 Bike Calories
  • 20 Power Cleans @ 95 lb
  • 10 C2 Bike Calories
  • 15 Bar-Facing Burpees
  • 20 Barbell Front-Rack Walking Lunges @ 95 lb
  • 30 Toes-to-Bar
  • 1,000m Row
  • Time Cap: 20 minutes

Stimulus: A long, flowing chipper with a fast and punchy bike-barbell-bike middle section.

Goal: Stay controlled through the opening half and build speed through the middle before pushing the return trip.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 4 rounds for quality
  • 10–12 DB Bench Press
  • 10–12 Strict Pull-Ups
  • 15 GHD Hip Extensions
  • 30–45 sec Heavy Sandbag Bear-Hug Hold

Accessory Intent: Build upper-body strength and posterior-chain endurance without adding excessive metabolic fatigue.
