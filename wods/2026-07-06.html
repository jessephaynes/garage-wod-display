Part A — Warm-Up

  • 3 rounds
  • 250m Echo Row
  • 10 PVC or empty-bar pass-throughs
  • 8 Empty-Bar Muscle Cleans
  • 8 Empty-Bar Front Squats
  • 6 Burpees
  • 20 Single-Unders or 10 Double-Unders

Part B — Strength

  • EMOM x 15
  • Minute 1: 2 Power Cleans @ 165 lb
  • Minute 2: 2 Power Cleans @ 175 lb
  • Minute 3: 2 Power Cleans @ 185 lb
  • Repeat for 5 cycles

Focus: Explosive turnover and crisp receiving position.

Intent: Build speed and consistency without approaching a max effort.

Part C — MetCon

  • 5 rounds for time
  • 12/10 Echo Bike Calories
  • 9 Power Cleans @ 135 lb
  • 12 Box Jump Overs (24 in)
  • 6 Bar Muscle-Ups
  • Scale to 8 Chest-to-Bar Pull-Ups if needed
  • Time Cap: 18 minutes

Stimulus: Fast-paced mixed-modal sprint with manageable barbell loading.

Goal: Finish in 12–16 minutes while keeping rounds consistent.

Part D — Accessory (Optional)

  • 3 rounds
  • 15 GHD Hip Extensions
  • 12 GHD Sit-Ups
  • 1:00 Front Rack Stretch
  • 1:00 Lat Hang from Pull-Up Bar

Accessory Intent: Build posterior chain endurance and restore upper-body mobility.
